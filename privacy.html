隱私權政策

lilizCue · 生效日期 2026 年 11 月 10 日

簡單說

lilizCue 不收集任何資料。App 沒有帳號系統、沒有廣告、沒有分析或追蹤工具，也沒有自己的伺服器。你的講稿、錄音與影片只保存在你的 iPhone 上，我們看不到，也不會上傳到任何第三方。

講稿與影片

你貼上、匯入或編輯的講稿，以及錄好的影片，都存在這支 iPhone 上 lilizCue 的資料夾裡。從「檔案」匯入講稿時使用系統的檔案挑選器，App 只會拿到你選的那一個檔案。只有在你按「存到相簿」或「分享」時，影片才會交給你所選擇的地方（例如「照片」、訊息、郵件），之後如何處理依該 App 或服務的政策。

相機與麥克風

相機與麥克風只在錄影畫面使用，用來錄下你的影片與聲音，並讓提詞跟著你的聲音捲動。

語音辨識

語音跟讀使用 Apple 在裝置上執行的語音辨識，聲音在手機上即時處理，不會上傳到我們的伺服器，也不會傳給其他第三方。第一次使用時，iOS 可能會從 Apple 下載中文語音辨識模型，這由 iOS 系統處理。

照片

相簿權限只用來把你選擇儲存的影片加入「照片」。lilizCue 不會讀取、上傳或分析你相簿裡的照片與影片。

App 內購買

訂閱（月繳、年繳）與一次買斷都由 Apple 的 App Store 處理付款。我們不會取得你的付款資訊，App 只會從 Apple 得知你是否擁有 Pro。購買綁定你的 Apple 帳號，可在同一帳號的裝置上恢復。

當機與診斷資訊

如果你在 iPhone 設定中選擇與開發者分享分析資料，或在 TestFlight 中回報問題，Apple 可能會提供當機報告等匿名診斷資訊給我們，用來修正錯誤。這由 Apple 依你的設定處理，你可以隨時在 iOS「設定 › 隱私權與安全性 › 分析與改進功能」中關閉。

兒童

App 不以兒童為對象，也不會收集兒童的個人資料。

刪除資料

你可以在 App 內刪除任何講稿或錄影。刪除 App 會一併刪除 App 內所有資料；若你的 iPhone 有 iCloud 或電腦備份，備份中可能仍含有 App 資料。已存到「照片」的影片需要在「照片」中刪除。

政策變更

若本政策有變更，會更新此頁並修改生效日期。

聯絡我們

有任何問題或建議，請寄信到 pericprew@gmail.com，我們會盡快回覆。

Privacy Policy

lilizCue · Effective November 10, 2026

In short

lilizCue does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and no server of our own. Your scripts, audio and videos are stored only on your iPhone; we cannot see them, and they are never uploaded to us or to any third party.

Scripts and videos

Scripts you paste, import or edit, and the videos you record, are stored in lilizCue's folder on your iPhone. When you import a script from Files, the system file picker gives the app only the file you choose. A video leaves the app only when you tap Save to Photos or Share, and it then goes to the place you chose (for example Photos, Messages or Mail), under that app's or service's own policy.

Camera and microphone

The camera and microphone are used only on the recording screen, to record your video and voice and to let the prompter follow your voice.

Speech recognition

Voice-follow uses Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your voice is processed on the phone in real time and is not sent to us or to any other third party. The first time, iOS may download Apple's Chinese speech model; this is handled by iOS.

Photos

Photo library access is used only to add the videos you choose to save to Photos. lilizCue never reads, uploads or analyses the photos and videos in your library.

In-app purchases

Subscriptions (monthly, yearly) and the lifetime purchase are paid through Apple's App Store. We never receive your payment details; the app only learns from Apple whether you own Pro. Purchases belong to your Apple Account and can be restored on devices that use it.

Crash and diagnostic information

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, or report a problem in TestFlight, Apple may provide us with anonymous diagnostics such as crash reports, which we use to fix bugs. Apple handles this according to your settings, and you can turn it off at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

You can delete any script or recording in the app. Deleting the app deletes all of its data; iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone may still contain app data. Videos you saved to Photos must be deleted in Photos.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the effective date revised.

Contact

Questions or feedback? Email pericprew@gmail.com and we will get back to you.
