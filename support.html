提詞沒有跟著我捲動？

請確認已允許麥克風與語音辨識（iPhone「設定 → lilizCue」）。第一次使用時，iOS 可能需要先下載中文語音辨識模型，請保持網路連線幾分鐘。說話時離手機近一點、環境安靜一些會更準。如果一直跟不上，可以在錄影前切換成「定速」或「手動」模式。

念錯字或臨時改詞會怎樣？

lilizCue 用讀音比對你念到哪裡，同音字、少念幾個字都跟得上。跳過一段時，它會在後面幾行內找到你；回頭重念也會跟著退回去。

講稿要怎麼加換氣和重點？

在講稿裡用「／」表示換氣、「／／」表示長停頓、用 **兩個星號** 包住重點，開頭寫「# 」可以當段落標籤。編輯畫面的工具列也可以直接點選加入。

影片存在哪裡？

錄好的影片先存在 App 裡，只有你按「存到相簿」或「分享」才會離開 App。換手機前請先把需要的影片存到相簿。

怎麼恢復或取消訂閱？

恢復：在 App 的「設定 → 恢復購買」或付費頁按「恢復購買」。取消：iPhone「設定 → 你的名字 → 訂閱 → lilizCue」，取消後可以使用到當期結束。

The prompter does not follow my voice

Make sure microphone and speech recognition are allowed (iPhone Settings → lilizCue). The first time, iOS may need to download its Chinese speech model, so stay online for a few minutes. Speaking closer to the phone in a quiet room helps. If it still lags, switch to 定速 (steady scroll) or 手動 (manual) mode before recording.

What if I misread or change a word?

lilizCue matches what you say by sound, so homophones and skipped words still keep up. If you jump ahead it finds you within the next few lines, and if you go back to re-read it follows you back.

Breath marks and highlights

In the script, type ／ for a breath, ／／ for a long pause, wrap key words in **double asterisks**, and start a line with "# " for a section label. The editor toolbar can add these for you.

Where are my videos?

Recordings stay inside the app until you tap Save to Photos or Share. Save the videos you need to Photos before changing phones.

Restore or cancel a subscription

Restore: tap 恢復購買 (Restore Purchases) in the app's Settings or on the subscription screen. Cancel: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → lilizCue; you keep access until the end of the current period.
