lilizCue

語音跟讀提詞機 · Voice-following Teleprompter

貼上講稿，自動排成好讀的提詞；打開前鏡頭錄影，你一開口，提詞就跟著你往下捲，停下來它就等你。不用註冊帳號，講稿和影片都留在你的 iPhone 上。

  • 用聲音對位：辨識結果寫成同音字，提詞照樣跟得上
  • 中文排版：自動斷句、不讓標點落在行首，可加換氣記號與重點標示
  • 提詞就在鏡頭正下方，眼神自然對著觀眾
  • 語音跟讀、定速捲動、手動三種模式，直式、橫式、方形都能錄
  • 語音辨識在裝置上完成，聲音不會上傳

使用 lilizCue 需要訂閱 Pro：月繳或年繳（都有 7 天免費試用），或一次買斷。實際價格以 App Store 顯示為準。

English

Paste your script and it is laid out as easy-to-read prompter lines. Record with the front camera and the text scrolls as you speak, and waits when you pause. No sign-up — your scripts and videos stay on your iPhone.

  • Follows by sound, so homophone mistakes by the recognizer still keep up
  • Chinese typesetting: automatic line breaks, no punctuation at the start of a line, breath marks and highlights
  • The prompter sits right under the camera, so your eyes stay on the viewer
  • Voice-follow, steady scroll and manual modes; portrait, landscape and square
  • Speech recognition runs on the device. Your voice is never uploaded

lilizCue requires a Pro subscription: monthly or yearly (both with a 7-day free trial), or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the App Store.
